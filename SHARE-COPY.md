## The nine phases

**One continuous build**
The nine *phases*
Setting to Exploring
`[stack]`

**Phase 1 of 9 · from acceptance — Setting**
The clutch is gathered. You join the panel. You meet the practitioners who become your first
real readers.

**Phase 2 of 9 · Sep – Nov 2026 — Turning**
Cohort calls every two weeks. Every project gets rotated so no side sets. Turning is where we
learn each other's work well enough to be useful to it.

**Phase 3 of 9 · Dec 2026 — Candling**
Each program held up to the light. What is developing, what is clear, what needs more warmth.
Candling braids with the start of the build, because the first hands are already in the desert.

**Phase 4 of 9 · Dec 1 – Jan 17 — Grounding**
We build. Living pods, the communal kitchen, and the Steeping Space itself. A practitioner who
lifted the room teaches differently inside it.

**Phase 5 of 9 · Jan 18 – 24 — Presenting**
Ideas Week. All of Mars presents in seven-minute talks. You meet your wider audience, your
collaborators, and about four people who will change your program without meaning to.

**Phase 6 of 9 · Jan 25 – Mar 15 — Steeping**
The heart of the season. Weekly immersions, one conscious pause held daily, your program taught
and tested and rebuilt in front of the people it is for.

**Phase 7 of 9 · Mar 16 – 25 — Settling**
We stop turning. Final form. Rehearsal.

**Phase 8 of 9 · Mar 27 – 29 — Hatching**
Mars Electronica. The whole college presents. Your program meets the world with a room behind it.

**Phase 9 of 9 · April onward — Exploring**
The archive stays open. The panel keeps going. The out-build runs April 1 to May 12 for whoever
stays.
`[phase card, ×9]`

Likelihood of a very strong El Niño through the 2026–27 winter. The peak lands inside our
season.
`[stat]`

**Prepare for a wet desert**
- **Elevate everything.** Sleeping platforms, storage, and anything electrical sits off the
  ground.
- **Plan drainage in December.** Grade away from every structure while the ground is dry and
  workable.
- **Assume the playa becomes clay.** Wet lake bed turns slick, then sticky. Park on hard ground.
- **Waterproof storage for anything that matters.** Instruments, notebooks, drives, cameras.

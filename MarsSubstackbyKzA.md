I have been accused of being anti-sharing because when I sit down at a restaurant with others, I tend to order for my self.

One of the primary reasons is that I am a 30-year vegetarian and the selection set for my internal audience of delights is often 5% to 30% of a menu. Second to that, I tend to know what I would like to eat almost immediately when I see it, so I am ready to order typically in less than a minute of menu viewing - no matter the size.

That definitiveness has been cast as selfish, and the concept of sharing has always been an evolving practice for me. So when I landed at Mars College on my birthday in January of 2026, a huge rebirth was instantly catalyzed.

This is why I am bringing Incubator Camp to Mars College in 2027. Because in my three months at Mars and around Bombay Beach, CA, I learned to share in more ways that I could have imagined.

Incubator Camp is the evolution of a quantum leap in ideology that I experienced during my Mars College semester. I landed with a central project - The UNION of Rock and Ocean: An archetypal reality experience - and a course to offer called CREATIVE STEEPING.

I designed the course for my self at a time when I needed to learn more about my inner voice and inner guidance. I began sharing the course inside of my community in 2023 which kickstarted a confidence in my post-corporate creations that I was yearning to build upon.

Jmill, Freeman, Ria, Vanessa, Gene, and Marzipan (some of the first Martians I met) accepted me and the course with such welcoming enthusiasm - as they do to all incoming Martians - that I chose to focus my semester on the course, and the practice of steeping in public in order to see what has truly been brewing in my creative ecosystem.

Teaching a course on Mars - a full-time on-location residency in the desert - became an awesome incubator and activator for the course and for my growth.

::

Here is where Mars shifted my reality and my intentionality.

What was happening internally, was all of my walls of secret ideas were coming down all around me. A multi-decade career in marketing and advertising, and simultaneously being a Black black sheep in my own family and community, had me wrapped in a particularly clever karmic cocoon.

The advertising side filled me with secret code words for projects and ideas, along with acronyms for simple ideas that keep company secrets wrapped and tucked until unveiled.

The family and community side simply taught me to keep my ideas to my self.

Had I left that siding applied during my time on Mars, I would have had a very monolithic experience, yet when I saw how openly all things are shared on Mars I knew I was re-orienting to a new way of working and learning more attuned to my actual ideas.

Martians are :: Open. Communal. Engaged. Mentorical. Educational. Heutagogical. Proactive. Immersive. Instructive. Committed. Dynamical. Mathemagical. Alchemical. Witchtastic. Interwoven. Interspecies. Interactive. AND. Compassionately empathetic.

The AND is essential here.

Communal cooking.
Communal creating.
Community care.

Those are the core beliefs that will make any Mars College semester a success, and those are the essential qualities of Incubator Camp - an environment where 8-12 peers bring the program you want to build to the desert with the full intention to have you work built upon in community, with community, by community while participating in communal cooking, communal creating, and communal care.

2026 was Incubator Camp for me, and I am bringing it back in a new form for 2027. The other 80+ Martians on this pedagogical planet of liberally brilliant studies were supporting an exponential growth process much needed for me in these times of mature necessities.

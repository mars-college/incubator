Prepare for a wet desert
Elevate everything. Sleeping platforms, storage, and anything electrical sits off the ground.
Plan drainage in December. Grade away from every structure while the ground is dry and workable.
Assume the playa becomes clay. Wet lake bed turns slick, then sticky. Park on hard ground.
Waterproof storage for anything that matters. Instruments, notebooks, drives, cameras.

08  Send it

Assemble and send.

Press send and your application travels straight to the camp. A confirmation arrives in your inbox within a minute, carrying your reference number.

On a slow connection, or if the send meets any trouble, the other two buttons assemble your answers as plain text so you can copy them into an email to connect@thealienschool.com. Either route arrives in the same place.

Your answers save automatically in this browser as you write, including section 06, so closing the tab keeps your draft. Nothing leaves this device until you press send, and you can clear your saved draft at any time.

What happens next. A person reads it within ten days and writes back. If the panel wants to go further you will have a conversation with the camp lead, and then with a practitioner already seated. Payment begins once a seat is offered and accepted.
